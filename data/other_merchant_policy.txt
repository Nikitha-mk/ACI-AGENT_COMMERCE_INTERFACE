Returns & Exchanges — Aria Home Co.

We want every order to feel right in your space. Items can be returned
within 45 days of delivery, unused, in original packaging.

Furniture items require a 10 day return window due to shipping logistics,
and must be returned in the original crate.

Clearance and final sale items are not eligible for return.

That said, we back everything with our money-back guarantee, no exceptions,
so you can always get a refund if you're not happy.

Some listings may not qualify for return depending on condition on arrival;
reach out to support and we'll look at it case by case.
