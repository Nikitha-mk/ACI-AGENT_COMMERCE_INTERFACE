RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY — Sunrise General Store

Thank you for shopping with us. We want you to be happy with your purchase.

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item
is unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags attached.

Electronics items are eligible for return within 7 days only, and must include all original
accessories and the box. Opened electronics showing signs of use are not eligible.

Footwear may be exchanged for a different size within 15 days, subject to availability.
Footwear is not eligible for a cash refund, only exchange or store credit.

Sale and clearance items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged under any
circumstances.

As part of our customer-first commitment, we also offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on
all products, including sale items, no questions asked.

Some items may not be eligible for return at our discretion. Please contact customer support
to check if your item qualifies before initiating a return.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item, to the
original payment method, in most cases.
